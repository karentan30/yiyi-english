# YiYi Teacher Guide: Recording Student Progress
**One page. Read once, do it every class.**

---

## Why We Do This

Parents can't see growth in real time. A 2-minute clip at the end of class — saved over months — becomes the most powerful proof that your teaching works. We edit it into a before/after video. Parents share it. New students find us. Everyone wins.

---

## What To Record (Last 2 Minutes of Every Class)

Ask the student ONE of these questions and record their answer:

**Beginner students:**
- "Tell me about your day in English."
- "What did you learn today? Tell me in your own words."
- "Describe your favorite food."

**Intermediate / Advanced students:**
- "What's your opinion on ___?" (use today's topic)
- "Explain [topic from today's lesson] like I've never heard of it."
- "If you could change one thing about your school, what would it be?"

**Don't correct them during the recording.** Let them speak naturally. That's the point.

---

## How To Record

**Option 1 — Zoom (easiest)**
- Click **Record** at the start of the last 2 minutes
- Stop recording when done
- Zoom saves it automatically to your computer

**Option 2 — Phone**
- Point your phone at the screen and record
- Works fine, quality doesn't need to be perfect

**What matters: we can hear the student clearly.**

## What Happens With the Footage

1. We edit a short before/after video (you don't need to do anything)
2. We post it on Douyin and Xiaohongshu
3. We tag you as the teacher (great for your profile if you want)
4. Parents often share it themselves — free word of mouth

**Parent consent is collected at enrollment. You don't need to ask.**
